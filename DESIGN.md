# Design System: Log Watchdog

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Incident workbench"**

A precise, restrained operations workspace. Compact rows, clear labels, and stable context support repeated scanning and investigation. The selected direction uses cool neutral work surfaces, restrained status fills, and visible separation between related regions.

The reusable signature is context-preserving selection: choosing an item exposes its evidence without losing the surrounding investigation. Data and readable controls carry the interface; decorative imagery has no established role. Motion communicates selection and state changes without interrupting reading. Exact motion values remain unresolved.

## Colors

Restrained neutrals with one action accent, as established by the selected direction:

- **Primary — workbench blue** (`#246b87`): actions and selected context.
- **Neutral — cool ground** (`#f4f6f7`): workspace background.
- **Neutral — panel white** (`#ffffff`): content surfaces.
- **Neutral — slate ink** (`#202c36`): primary text.

Severity, warning, success, border, focus, and disabled colors: **[to be resolved during implementation]**. Status colors supplement explicit labels; they never carry meaning alone. These are direction swatches, not extracted or contrast-verified tokens.

## Typography

Readable operational labels and compact data take precedence over display styling. Keep timestamps, counts, and percentages aligned, with consistent units and precision.

Font families, sizes, weights, line heights, and numeric font settings: **[to be resolved during implementation]**.

## Layout

Use a stable workspace with compact ruled rows and adjacent contextual regions on desktop. Preserve the task's reading order when regions stack on narrow screens. Keep secondary detail progressively disclosed and table overflow contained.

Exact grid dimensions, spacing scale, row heights, and breakpoints: **[to be resolved during implementation]**. The console's specific composition and navigation contract live in its surface brief and `docs/ui-flow.md`.

## Elevation & Depth

The selected direction distinguishes regions through neutral surface tones, restrained fills, and rules. Shadow values and overlay elevation: **[to be resolved during implementation]**.

## Shapes

Use compact, consistent controls and restrained boundaries suited to dense operational content. Exact corner radii, border widths, and icon treatment: **[to be resolved during implementation]**.

## Do's and Don'ts

- **Do** prioritize scanability, explicit severity labels, and useful trends.
- **Do** preserve selected context while evidence and background data update.
- **Do** pair status color with readable text and accessible state semantics.
- **Don't** let decorative treatment compete with investigation evidence.
- **Don't** present these seed values as an implemented or verified design system.
